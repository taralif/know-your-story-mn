Twin Cities Jewish teens, 2026–27

Help teens talk about the hard stuff.

Know Your Story brings 8th–12th graders together for three Sundays of learning with StandWithUs. After each session, teens break into grade-level groups to actually talk about it. We're hiring the people who lead those conversations.

Two roles

Both work with the same teens on the same three Sundays. They do different jobs in the room.

Group Facilitator

$1,000 for the project

For experienced Jewish educators. Four spots, covering grades 8–9, 10–11, and 12.

  • Lead a grade-level discussion group for 30–45 minutes at each session
  • Attend both trainings with StandWithUs educators
  • Help teens process what they just learned — not deliver a lesson
  • Hold the room when a conversation gets uncomfortable, which it will

College-Age Mentor

$550 for the project

For college students, including Hillel students at the U. A few spots.

  • Join the breakout groups as a near-peer, not a second teacher
  • Attend both trainings
  • Bring energy, ask the question a teen is afraid to ask
  • Be someone a 15-year-old can picture themselves becoming

What we're looking for

We're not asking for a resume. We're asking how you are in a room with teenagers.

Matters a lot

  • You've led a group of teens before — camp, youth group, Hillel, classroom, anywhere
  • You can sit with a hard question instead of rushing to answer it
  • You're clear about your own Jewish and Zionist identity, and comfortable when a teen isn't yet
  • Teens like being around you

Matters less

  • Years of experience
  • Formal teaching credentials
  • Deep expertise in Israeli history — StandWithUs handles the content
  • Having every answer

This is not a teaching job. StandWithUs runs the learning portion of each session. Your job starts when the teens break into groups and have to figure out what they actually think.
