Questions

Real quick.

Do I need to come to all three?

Yes, that's the plan! Each session builds on the last, so sign up for all three. If you have to miss one, still come to the others.

Do I need to know a lot already?

Nope. It works whether you've had years of Jewish school or almost none. No one's going to quiz you.

Are you going to tell me what to think?

No. We won't hand you talking points. You'll get the history and the facts, then work through the hard questions with people your age, so you can figure out what you think.

Is this a lecture?

It's interactive. A StandWithUs educator gets things started, then you break into small groups by grade with facilitators and college-age mentors.

How do I sign up?

Tap any "Sign up" button. It takes about a minute on Luma, and you or a parent can do it. Questions? Email knowyourstorymn@gmail.com.

What about my parents?

We're running a parent group alongside the teen sessions. Send them the main page.
